## Practice: parcel prices
Read a nonnegative integer weight in grams.
Set `cost` to 3 for weights up to and including 100, to 5 up to and including 500, and to 9 above 500.
Print only the cost on the final line.
Use `int(input(...))` to read the number and an `if`/`elif`/`else` chain to choose the cost.
